# Smart Link — Pilot Call Notes Template

Date: 2026-05-30
Scope: first pilot conversations only. This is a lightweight notes template for the first 5-shop outreach wave, not a baby CRM wearing fake glasses.

## How to use this
- Make one copy of the "Call notes card" section per prospect.
- Keep answers short and concrete.
- Capture what the owner actually said, not what we wish they meant.
- If a new feature request appears, log it under requested changes and move on.

## Follow-up status options
- `new` — no conversation yet
- `contacted` — outreach sent, waiting for reply
- `interested` — owner wants more info or a demo
- `call_booked` — call/demo scheduled
- `pilot_candidate` — good fit, waiting on assets or approval
- `not_now` — soft no, follow up later
- `declined` — hard no

## Source tag options
Use the same source language as the outreach packet when possible:
- `instagram_dm`
- `email`
- `sms`
- `whatsapp`
- `phone_followup`
- `referral`
- `walk_in`
- `other:<write it>`

## Call notes card

### Prospect snapshot
- Business name:
- Area / borough:
- Segment: `barbershop` | `salon`
- Source tag:
- Contact channel used:
- Contact value (phone/email/handle):
- Date of conversation:
- Operator:

### Current booking workflow
- How do they handle bookings today? (phone, DM, walk-in, booking app, mixed)
- Where do customers usually find them? (Google, Instagram, referrals, walk-ins, etc.)
- What is messy or slow in the current process?

### Owner objections
- Main hesitation or objection:
- Exact phrasing worth remembering:
- Is the objection about trust, time, price, complexity, or something else?

### Setup friction
- Missing assets or info: (services, prices, hours, photos, booking URL, approval contact)
- Operational blockers: (staff availability, owner too busy, no one can approve, etc.)
- Tech blockers: (no website, weak phone habits, no Instagram access, etc.)

### Willingness to pay
- Reaction to free 30-day pilot:
- Reaction to post-pilot pricing:
- What outcome would make them pay?
- Budget signal: `none` | `low` | `medium` | `high`

### Requested changes
- Feature or workflow requests they asked for:
- Which request sounds actually important vs. just nice-to-have?
- Anything that should become a separate product card later?

### Overall fit and momentum
- Best sign of interest:
- Biggest risk to closing this pilot:
- Follow-up status:
- Next action:
- Next action due date:

- Freeform notes, quotes, weird details, and anything you do not want to lose:
